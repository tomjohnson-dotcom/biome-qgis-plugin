EPSL stylised species icons
===========================

Game-icons.net artwork is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Sources used:
  - Bat by Skoll, game-icons.net/1x1/skoll/bat.html
  - Dolphin by Delapouite, adapted for Harbour porpoise,
    game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/dolphin.html
  - Frog by Lorc, adapted for Natterjack toad
  - Gecko by Lorc, adapted for Sand lizard
  - Butterfly by Lorc, adapted for Marsh fritillary
  - Sprout by Lorc, adapted for Plant

The dormouse face is a simplified monochrome trace of the CC0 Mouse Face
reference published by SVG Repo:
https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/402178/mouse-face

The full-body otter is adapted from the CC0 Otter Facing Right silhouette
published by SVG Repo:
https://www.svgrepo.com/svg/14227/otter-facing-right

The water-vole mark is adapted from the Material Design Icons ``rodent``
symbol by Pictogrammers, licensed under Apache 2.0 and distributed by Iconify.

The crayfish mark is adapted from the Fluent Emoji High Contrast ``lobster``
symbol by Microsoft, licensed under MIT and distributed by Iconify.

The Great crested newt badge reuses Biome's established ecological-feature
symbol. The mixed-reptile badge reuses the smooth-snake mark with a distinct
colour. Remaining simplified silhouettes and the neutral fallback were drawn
for Biome.

All source artwork has been simplified, recoloured to white on a coloured
badge, scaled, and re-centred for use as small QGIS map markers.
